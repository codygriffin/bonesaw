Architecture decision

Effort may persist; authority may not leak
A private request mask remembers the one relocking leg. The WBC still receives only measured raw/stable/hard support, and RollingWheel still requires force-backed qualification.
Preload is model-relative and screened
Nine frozen targets span zero to 1.4 mm preload below the 50 mm tire radius. Exactly 0.9 mm passes mirrored requalification, terminal guardrail, and admission gates.
Repeated relock is not recovery
Both 6 N signs relock three times, then fall after the later pulse. The result changes the next problem from “make contact” to “reject accumulated body moment before the third loss.”

R310 remains the public controller. R313 is default-off and changes no reset, timeout, solver iteration budget, or measured-contact authority.
