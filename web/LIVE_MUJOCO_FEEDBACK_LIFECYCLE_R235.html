Lifecycle contract

CommandGuaranteed behavior
plant_pauseClears the active wrench lease, freezes MuJoCo time/qpos/qvel, skips WBC and physics, and continues a heartbeat/state stream. Push is rejected while paused.
plant_resumeRestarts from the frozen measured state. No previous wrench is resurrected.
plant_resetRebuilds authored balanced standing, clears the wrench, increments reset_epoch, and preserves paused ownership if reset was requested while paused.
