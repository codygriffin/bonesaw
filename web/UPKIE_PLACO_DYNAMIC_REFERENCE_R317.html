Upkie floating reference comparison · R317

Result

This is the first isolated full-body Upkie comparison against PlaCo's floating inverse-dynamics implementation. Both workers consume the same 256 frozen states and acceleration targets; policy, integration, simulator, and physics rollout are all absent. Bonesaw retains its Rust nonholonomic rolling-contact model. PlaCo uses its independently implemented unilateral material-point contacts at the two wheel centers.

**Reference availability: PASS. Exact controller parity: NOT CLAIMED.** The contact laws are intentionally visible and materially different, so output divergence is evidence rather than a failure hidden by tuning.
