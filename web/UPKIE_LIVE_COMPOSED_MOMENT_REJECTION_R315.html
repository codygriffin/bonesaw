Architecture correction

Application-body inertial moment
Preserved for compatible operator/UI telemetry and lever validation. It is not silently reused as a generalized wrench.
Aggregate-CoM moment
Reserved for the independently switchable centroidal angular-momentum objective. That soft objective is zero in this screen.
Root-body-origin moment + force
Matches the Rust generalized tangent [root angular; root-origin linear; joints] and alone shifts the six floating dynamics equalities one completed control tick later.
Fail-closed consequence
Nonadmission retains prior admitted effort for that tick; it does not reset, invent contact, extend the iteration budget, or turn a failed solve into executable authority.
